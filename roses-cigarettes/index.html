Famous model Jane Rosefield's attempted suicide, though unsuccessful, draws the attention of a prominent fashion magazine, which subsequently publishes an article about her. The captivating cover photo of Jane sparks the curiosity of a young boy named Dave. Intrigued by her enigmatic presence, Dave becomes fascinated and resolves to delve into her world, uncovering more about her life and story.
